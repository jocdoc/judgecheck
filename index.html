    JudgeCheck is a statistical screening tool, not proof of misconduct. It measures how unusual a scoring pattern is compared to chance; it cannot know why a pattern exists. Results should prompt review by the appropriate governing body — never punishment on their own. Uploaded files are processed in memory and are not stored. PDF extraction can make mistakes — always check the preview table against your source document before running the analysis.
